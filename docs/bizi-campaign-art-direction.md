# Bizi campaign art

Paint Constanti in sandy sepia ink and transparent watercolor. Use pale limestone and aged brass for the broad shapes, with small areas of transit blue and tile red. A single legible action should identify the chapter on a mission tile.

## Sentient trains

Bizi citizens include both upright automata and individuals whose bodies are locomotives. Show a train making a choice a player can see. Its swiveling optic can meet another citizen’s gaze, its articulated coupler can offer assistance, or its signal arm can grant another train passage. Give each locomotive a chassis suited to its work. Preserve the established body of each named character.

## The Titans

Machina, Melech, and Meca belong to Bizi’s own religious history. Machina is associated with making, Melech with law and guidance, and Meca with command. In *The First Titan*, Machina’s ancient ribbed blue-and-brass engine dwarfs the newly awakened workers. In *The Three Titans*, the maker at its tools, the upright lawgiver, and the domed command engine each occupy a distinct apse. Keep all three silhouettes legible at campaign-map size.
