# Legacies: Gracus and Indela draft rules

These implementations translate the shared Gauntlet Card Registry drafts into the deterministic two-player rules engine. The published in-game descriptions are authoritative while the abilities remain marked as drafts.

## Gracus

- **Epicura, Voice of the Arena — Call the Minotaur:** once per turn during priority, create a 4-value Minotaur as an attacker or as a blocker for an unblocked attack targeting you. Attacking Minotaurs can be blocked normally. Tokens leave the match after combat rather than entering a discard pile.
- **Athun, Coastline of Giants — Towering Follow-up:** a card played at least 8 printed value above the last card its controller played that turn gets +2 value.
- **Platus — Demand Tribute:** attacks targeting the Gracus player require one additional payment. The engine enforces this before the attack can be declared.

## Indela

- **Katel, Magus Operandi — Opening Omen:** when the Indela player receives starting priority for a turn, reveal the top card of their deck. An odd omen reduces that player's attack and block costs by 1; an even omen raises the opponent's attack and block costs by 1.
- **Kashi, Academy of Omens — Opposing Parity:** cards with parity opposite a revealed omen get +1 value when played.
- **Ramar — Second Reading:** the shared draft repeats Katel's exact trigger and effect. Choosing Ramar therefore applies the omen twice, for a total cost adjustment of 2, while Kashi's value bonus remains +1.

Both factions receive the complete standard 52-card playing deck. Constructed replacements remain optional. Gracus and Indela are supported in practice, private two-player faction tables, and Faction Ranked; Classic Ranked remains unchanged.
