# Babylon ordinary-player playtest record

Use this record only with Play mode and the developer drawer closed. Do not
explain the board before the participant begins. The purpose is to test whether
the official match experience communicates Gauntlet through the battlefield
itself.

The production gate requires at least five completed records. Participants
must not be involved in implementation, at least three must be new to the
developer sandbox, and the set must include both desktop and touch users.
